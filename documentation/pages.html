Crypto

Page / routeImplemented behavior
Market
/crypto/market		Fixed sample prices, history range selection, movers, watchlist, sorting, and asset details.
Wallet
/crypto/wallet		Sample holdings, allocation, cost basis, and unrealized gain/loss. No real wallet connection.
Exchange
/crypto/exchange		Preview conversion and a sample 0.25% fee using fixed prices. No order is submitted.
Transactions
/crypto/transactions		Ten sample records, type/status filters, sorting, detail dialogs, and export; no transaction hashes or on-chain activity.

AI Analytics

Page / routeImplemented behavior
AI Insights
/ai-analytics/insights		Eight sample insights, discovery chart, category/status filters, saving, review state, evidence details, recommended actions, and CSV export.
Chatbot Assistant
/ai-analytics/chatbot-conversations		Eight sample conversations with user photos, search/channel/status filters, resolve/reopen/review flags, local reply previews, internal notes, and transcript export. No customer messages are sent.
Predictions
/ai-analytics/predictions		Six metrics, historical/forecast charts, 7/14/30-day horizons, scenario comparison, assumptions, and CSV export. Forecasts linearly scale a fixed sample trend; optimistic/adverse cases shift it by 8%. Bounds are illustrative +/-12% ranges, not statistical confidence intervals.
Model Settings
/ai-analytics/model-settings		Six demo profiles, activation switches, generation/review/runtime/instruction tabs, validation/reset/save, local activity, and JSON export. Temperature 0-1, tokens 256-8192, threshold 50-100, timeout 5-120 seconds, retries 0-3; instructions up to 2000 characters. No live model settings change.

School Management

Page / routeImplemented behavior
Students
/school/students		Sample directory, profile details, admission form, enrollment status changes, class filters, and export.
Classes
/school/classes		Class groups, teachers, rooms, sample enrollment, capacity, and roster dialogs.
Attendance
/school/attendance		Per-date local registers, distribution chart, class completion, attention list, notes/late minutes, selected-student bulk marking, unmarked marking, and undo. Rate = present + late divided by present + late + absent; excused/unmarked are excluded. Late minutes accept 0-240.
Exams
/school/exams		Sample exam schedule, creation, details, and cancellation. Detects overlapping scheduled exams for the same class or venue; duration is 30-180 minutes.

HRM

Page / routeImplemented behavior
Employees
/hrm/employees		Grid/list directory, team/work-arrangement/status filters, sorting, profile editing, manager/location/contact details, local photos, duplicate-email validation, and CSV export.
Payroll
/hrm/payroll		Sample salary/allowance/deduction breakdown and processed status. No tax calculation or payment is performed.
Leave Requests
/hrm/leave-requests		Weekly time-off calendar, priorities, filters/sorting, decision notes/history, same-department coverage check, and duplicate-date request validation. Rejection requires a note. Days are inclusive calendar days, not policy-adjusted working days.
Performance Reviews
/hrm/reviews		Create and edit drafts, ratings/feedback, completion, reopening, filters, and export. Completion requires feedback.

Hospital Management

Page / routeImplemented behavior
Patients
/hospital/patients		Fictional patient directory, details, local registration, status/category filters, and export.
Doctors
/hospital/doctors		Generated fictional portraits, grid/list directory, specialty/availability filters, saving, profile and schedule dialogs, local availability changes, and sample booking with duplicate doctor/patient slot checks. Bookings do not update the separate Appointments page.
Appointments
/hospital/appointments		Sample queue and booking, doctor/patient duplicate-time checks, and scheduled → checked-in → completed transitions.
Wards & Beds
/hospital/wards		Three-floor room layout, map/list, occupancy, bed assignment/transfer, cleaning queue, maintenance states, notes/activity, and export. Transfer marks the source bed cleaning and assigns the patient to the destination. No real admissions system is updated.
Pharmacy
/hospital/pharmacy		Inventory catalog, batch/expiry/stock, low-stock filters, and local stock additions of 1-10000 whole units. No prescribing or dispensing workflow.
Ambulance
/hospital/ambulance		Fictional fleet cards and local simulated dispatch status. No vehicle is dispatched or emergency service contacted.
Billing
/hospital/billing		Sample itemized invoices and paid status. Net = consultation + services - adjustment; no payment is collected.
